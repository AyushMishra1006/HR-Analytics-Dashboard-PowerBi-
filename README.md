# HR-Analytics-Dashboard-PowerBi-


📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

A data-driven HR Analytics solution built using Power BI, designed to help organizations monitor attrition trends, workforce distribution, and employee demographics to improve decision-making.

🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee data to uncover key insights related to attrition, age groups, department-wise trends, salary impact, and education-wise attrition.
The dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of HR metrics that help HR teams understand employee turnover and workforce behavior.

🗂️ Key Features
1️⃣ Summary Metrics

At the top of the dashboard, key HR KPIs are displayed:

Total Employees: 63

Attrition Count: 12

Attrition Rate: 19.05%

Average Age: 38 years

Average Salary: ₹6.7K

Average Tenure: 7.2 years

These KPIs provide a quick glance at overall workforce health.

2️⃣ Visual Insights Included
📌 Attrition by Age Group

A bar chart showing how attrition varies across age brackets (25–35, 35–45, 45–55 etc.).
Helps identify the most vulnerable age segments.

📌 Job Role vs Attrition

A matrix displaying how many employees left from each job role.
Useful for recognizing role-specific turnover issues.

📌 Attrition by Salary Slab

Shows whether salary range impacts attrition (e.g., most attrition occurs in the lower salary slabs like <5k).

📌 Education Field vs Attrition

A donut chart representing attrition trends based on educational background (Human Resources, Life Sciences, Technical Degree, etc.).

📌 Attrition by Department & Gender

A segment comparing attrition between male vs female employees within departments.

📌 Attrition Over Tenure

A line + area chart showing attrition count based on Years at Company, helping identify risky tenure periods.

🎯 Insights Identified

Some major insights visible from the dashboard:

Highest attrition occurs among employees earning less than ₹5k.

Age group 25–35 shows maximum attrition.

Human Resources department has the highest attrition share.

Attrition is almost equal for males and females (6 each).

Employees with 1–3 years of experience show higher turnover.

This helps HR teams act proactively on compensation, retention planning, and engagement strategies.
